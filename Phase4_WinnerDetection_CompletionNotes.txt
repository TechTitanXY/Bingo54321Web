54321-BINGO!
PHASE 4 — WINNER DETECTION AND CELEBRATION
Completion Notes
November 25, 2026

==================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================

Phase 4 introduced automatic Bingo winner detection, a Winners panel, audible winner celebrations, visual highlighting, and automatic game-completion handling.

The implementation was tested using permanent saved Bingo cards A001 through A004.

==================================================
FEATURES IMPLEMENTED
==================================================

1. Automatic Winner Detection
   - Evaluates all 12 standard Bingo winning lines:
     - 5 horizontal rows
     - 5 vertical columns
     - 2 diagonals
   - Treats the center FREE square as automatically marked.
   - Evaluates only registered cards.
   - Recognizes multiple winners on the same called number.
   - Records each winning Card ID only once.
   - Preserves the winning number and order of winners.

2. Winners Panel
   - Displays winning Card IDs and their winning calls.
   - Retains earlier winners throughout the game.
   - Highlights newly detected winners.
   - Supports simultaneous winners.

3. Audible Celebration
   - Uses synthesized bell sounds without external audio files.
   - Updated to five louder dings over approximately three seconds.
   - Includes a Bell On/Off control.
   - Bell celebration ends automatically.

4. Visual Celebration
   - Newly winning cards alternate between White and Gold backgrounds.
   - Dark lettering remains readable throughout the animation.
   - Highlighting continues until calling resumes.
   - Earlier winners remain visible without flashing.
   - Supports reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

5. Automatic Calling
   - Pauses whenever a new winner is detected.
   - Supports manual continuation and Resume Auto.
   - Prevents repeated announcements for previous winners.

6. Game Completion
   - Detects when all registered cards have won.
   - Disables Resume Auto and Call Next Number.
   - Preserves the final winner's flashing highlight.
   - Provides a game-completion message.
   - New Game is designed to reset called numbers and winners while retaining registrations.

7. The electronic bell was replaced with your original five-ding MP3 recording.
   - The application successfully played the five-ding MP3 recording.

8. Production Build and ESLint
   - Phase 4 was successfully built for production.
   - ESLint completed without errors or warnings.
